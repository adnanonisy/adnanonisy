# /brag plan — High Note Jordan (مس آلاء الشريف)

**What it is:** A four-book Advanced English series for Jordanian Tawjihi (Grade 12) by Ala'a Al Sharif: the full book, a 1474-question bank, meanings notes, and paragraph summaries.
**Who it's for:** Tawjihi students (and their parents) preparing for the ministry exam (الوزاري).
**What sets it apart:** It covers the whole course, "من أول كلمة… لآخر سؤال وزاري", with a separate book for each need: reading, exams, meanings and writing.
**Most impressive claim:** 1474 سؤال in the question bank.
**Visual hook:** A bubble answer sheet filling in, then "جاهز للوزاري؟" slammed on with a highlighter.
**Real material shown:** The four real cover mockups, and the covers' own copy (Every lesson, Every task, Every mark / 1474 سؤال / ضع دائرة / A GOOD PARAGRAPH / meanings notes).
**Tone:** `default` in the reference's language: cream notebook paper, lime highlighter, stickers, stamps. It's playful but tidy, with more motion and a real "product in use" beat for each book.
**Share caption:** From the first word to the last ministry question — the complete High Note Jordan pack for Tawjihi Advanced English.

## Angle
The reference video lines the books up. This version makes each book *do its job* on the page: the question bank counts up to 1474 and fills in bubbles, the paragraph book draws its mind map, the meanings notes flip English words to Arabic, and the full book writes its promise in ink. Then the tagline lands on the beat, and the package, name, phone and price close it out.

## Format
Landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 22s, with Arabic RTL typography (Lalezar display, Cairo body, Caveat for the English tagline).
Music: bundled "Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 1" (120 BPM, beats at 0.02 + 0.5n). The energy lift at 16.0s lands on the outro.
